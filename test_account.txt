Tenant Test Accounts (Template Only)
-----------------------------------
Do not store real usernames/passwords in this repository.

Use one of the following approaches instead:
1. Keep test credentials in your local `.env` (never committed).
2. Use a secrets manager (Vault / AWS Secrets Manager / etc.).
3. Share temporary credentials through a secure channel only.
